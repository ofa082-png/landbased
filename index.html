Feed, and the gap between the two ratios

Nobody publishes what they actually spent on feed in tonnes. Four of the six publish a feed conversion ratio, but the level is not comparable between them — accumulated FCR climbs as the fish grow, so a pool of 500 g fish will always flatter a farm harvesting at 3 kg. What is comparable is the distance between the biological ratio, which counts only the fish that lived, and the economic ratio, which counts all the feed against all the fish sold. That distance is the mortality and waste.

Where the growth went

Growth has to end up somewhere: sold as harvest, still swimming as standing biomass, or lost. The last bar is what the filings do not account for — shrink from fasting before slaughter, downgrades, a wrong gutted-weight yield, or a biomass estimate that was too high.
